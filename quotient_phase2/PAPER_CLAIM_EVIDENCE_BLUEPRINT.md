# Phase-II manuscript claims and evidence

The Phase-II manuscript evaluates adaptive sub-portal quadtree coarsening for repeated planning queries in bottlenecked discrete LMDPs. Performance statements use the clean-isolation measurements on the locked solver. [Manuscript](MANUSCRIPT_CORE_DRAFT.md) · [Primary records](m233_clean_isolation_replication.json)

| Manuscript claim | Evidence and scope |
| --- | --- |
| Goal elimination and policy bound | §§2–3 give the complete incoming goal ring, M-matrix comparison and reachability assumptions for the exact-arithmetic policy bound. §4 distinguishes these hypotheses from toleranced float64 verification. [Mechanism and boundary cases](m233_fig1_mechanism_and_counterexamples.png) |
| Adaptive refinement and response reuse | A single L0 witness is reused across nested cuts. PoU conservation uses at most k−1 child solves for a k-way split with the parent available. The measured maximum difference from direct child responses was 9.71 × 10⁻¹⁷. [Contract and PoU checks](m233_clean_isolation_replication.json) |
| Repeated-query static timing | The locked solver on 12 confirmation maps at 47³ saved 28.80% planner time against matrix-free exact Schur (11/12 faster, paired difference −2.959 s, 95% CI [−3.957, −1.962] s). The prespecified one-sided 20% threshold passed planner-only (p = 0.0292) and with GP plus assembly (28.03%, p = 0.0366). The clean protocol reused these confirmation maps. [Paired records](m233_clean_isolation_replication.json) |
| Replay and local update limits | With six replay maps per grid, the 35³, 47³ and 59³ static savings were 11.28%, 25.51% and 24.38%. All three 20% tests were inconclusive. The 35³ two-sided exact sign-flip p was 0.0625. Continual 47³ at six queries per update yielded 9.39% savings with CI spanning zero for marginal and lifecycle cost. [Table 2 and Appendix A](MANUSCRIPT_CORE_DRAFT.md) |
| Contribution of stored responses | On six replay maps, retaining L1–L4 responses as well as L0 saved 34.15% planner time and 57.34% operational room-LU right-hand sides against L0-only. The cold-query comparison gave 44.42% and 63.48%, respectively. [Table 4](MANUSCRIPT_CORE_DRAFT.md) |
| Global LU and memory measurements | The clean run timed a global LU numerical reference after both planner arms on one map per static block. The four matched measurements appear in §5.4. Factor and response-cache bytes are selected arrays. Process VmRSS was sampled at arm boundaries. [Per-map records](m233_clean_isolation_replication.json) |
| Verification denominator | All 2,304 method events passed the reference inequalities and all 792 scheduled policy checks passed with zero slack, across 900 distinct geometric tasks. The map is the statistical unit for timing. [Primary records](m233_clean_isolation_replication.json) |
