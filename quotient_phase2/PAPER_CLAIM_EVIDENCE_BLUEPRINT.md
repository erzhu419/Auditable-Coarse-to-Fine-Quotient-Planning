## 1. Manuscript Title, Abstract Framing, & Defensible Scope

### 1.1 Recommended Abstract Result Statement
> *In discrete Cole–Hopf KL-LMDPs on bottlenecked 3D multi-room domains, eliminating room interiors and pre-multiplying the doorway Schur operator by its block-diagonal in-plane inverse yields a non-negative integral portal operator whose runtime-verified 1-step Howard subsolution extends harmonically to a global subsolution and certifies an executable lazy-room policy within explicit IEEE-754 float64 tolerances. Across streaming single-room targets, persistent nested dyadic portal coarsening cuts planning time by `36%–58%` (`1.57x–2.39x`) relative to global `SingleLU` target-capacitance reuse (`79%` / `4.74x` under known-support local operator updates). Relative to a closest-neighbor matrix-free exact Schur baseline sharing identical room decompositions, local LU factors, and L0 Lyapunov scaling, adaptive portal coarsening provides no speedup on small `35^3` (`7x7`) doorways (`-12.2%`), but achieves statistically significant planning-time reductions on larger `47^3` (`9x9`) and `59^3` (`11x11`) doorways—`15.2%` (`p = 0.0174`) and `13.7%` (`p = 0.0051`) under exclusive single-worker `AB/BA` replay (`19.8%` and `17.6%` under 3-worker execution)—and `10.0%` (`p = 1.4e-4`) marginal savings under localized operator updates (`16.6%` under 3 workers), driven by a `29.8%` (`1.42x`) reduction over `L0`-only caching.*

   - **Three-Level Cache Mechanism Attribution (`M2.3.2 WP-2`, `47^3` `1`-Worker `2x` `3x3` Latin Square)**: Keeping intra-query memoization active across all modes, persistent `L0..L3` caching (`8.56s`, `9,322` local LU solves/map) cuts planning time by **`29.75%` (`1.42x`, $p = 1.20\times 10^{-4}$)** vs. `CacheL0Only` (`12.18s`, `19,009` solves) and **`43.98%` (`1.79x`, $p = 1.48\times 10^{-6}$)** vs. `QueryCold` (`15.28s`, `21,457` solves).

2. **Negative & Scope-Limiting Pillars (Main Text Section 6)**:

## 4. Positioning Against Closest Prior Literature

1. **Partitioned & Hierarchical LMDPs — Todorov (NIPS 2009); Saxe, Earle & Rosman (ICML 2017); Infante, Jonsson & Gómez (AAAI 2022, pp. 6970–6977, `arXiv:2106.15380`)**:
   - Infante et al. (2022) already establish room-partitioned LMDP boundary-value composition, implicit globally optimal primitive policies, and subtask sharing across identical partitions; Todorov (2009) introduced Green-function / SVD composition of control laws. We do **not** claim room partitioning or boundary composition as new.
   - Our specific contribution lies at the interface when doorways are multi-node 2D manifolds (`81–121` nodes/doorway, `6,171` total interface DOFs) with heterogeneous interior hazards: **(i)** block-diagonal doorway smoothing $K_{\Gamma\Gamma} = A_{\Gamma\Gamma}^{-1}(-M)$ + runtime-verified 1-step Howard subsolutions $W_{\text{lo}}$ that turn coarse piecewise-constant portal aggregates into valid global subsolutions ($A z_{\text{sub}} \le 0$) bounding deployed discrete KL-LMDP regret, **(ii)** nested adaptive dyadic portal coarsening + Lyapunov-scaled defect closure, and **(iii)** empirical cost-boundary isolation against `MatrixFree_ExactSchur_Krylov` and `SingleLU_GoalCapacitance`.
2. **Port-Reduced Static Condensation — Eftang & Patera (2013); Smetana & Patera (2016)**:
   - Extends port-reduced condensation from linear/energy-norm elliptic PDE outputs to **nonlinear log-value control readout ($V = -\lambda \log z$) and closed-loop stochastic policy loss bounds**, where order preservation and positivity are required across multi-room decay chains.
3. **MDP Lumpability — Givan, Dean & Greig (2003)**:
   - Constructs **query-validated upper/lower portal envelopes** under survival-normalized adequacy (`C12–C13`), without claiming a task-universal bisimulation quotient.
