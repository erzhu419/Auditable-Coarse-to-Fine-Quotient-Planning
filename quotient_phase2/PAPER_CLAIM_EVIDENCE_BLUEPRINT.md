## 4. Positioning Against Closest Prior Literature

1. **Hierarchical & Compositional LMDPs — Todorov (2009); Saxe, Earle & Rosman (ICML 2017); Infante, Jonsson & Gómez (AAAI 2022, *Globally Optimal Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning for Linearly-Solvable Markov Decision Processes*, pp. 6970–6977, `arXiv:2106.15380`)**:
   - *What prior work already establishes*: Todorov (2009) and Saxe et al. (2017) established linear compositionality of boundary desirability bases, and **Infante, Jonsson & Gómez (2022)** formulated partitioned multi-room LMDPs where subtask boundary value functions compose across room interfaces, induce an implicit globally optimal primitive policy without high-level non-stationarity, and share identical dynamics across repeated partitions. Thus, partitioning an LMDP into rooms + boundary portals and composing local Green responses is **not** claimed as our invention.
   - *Our concrete delta*: Infante et al. (2022) maintain uncoarsened boundary state sets (or exact partition equivalence) without finite-precision suboptimality certificates when high-dimensional 2D-manifold portals (`81–121` nodes per 3D doorway, `6,171` interface nodes total) are approximated. Direct piecewise-constant portal aggregation incurs an $O(1/h^2)$ doorway-seam singularity, while naive coarsening incurs up to `1.28` policy regret (`M2.3 Coarse_L0`). We introduce **(i)** block-diagonal doorway smoothing $K_{\Gamma\Gamma} = A_{\Gamma\Gamma}^{-1}(-M)$ + runtime-verified 1-step Howard subsolutions $W_{\text{lo}}$ that guarantee $A z_{\text{sub}} \le 0$ and certify explicit $\varepsilon$-regret bounds for the deployed primitive KL-LMDP policy, **(ii)** strictly nested task-driven dyadic portal coarsening + Lyapunov-scaled interface defect closure, and **(iii)** rigorous wall-clock attribution separating room elimination (`MatrixFree_ExactSchur_Krylov`) from portal coarsening & cross-task/local-update cache reuse (`M231_Portal_CacheOn`).
2. **Port-Reduced Static Condensation & Multiscale FEM — Eftang & Patera (2013); Smetana & Patera (2016)**:
   - Port-reduced static condensation introduced local harmonic transfer spaces and output error bounds for elliptic PDEs, and positivity-preserving ROM variants exist in specialized PDE settings. Our contribution specifically targets **log-transformed stochastic control readout ($V = -\lambda \log z$) and closed-loop Markov policy certification**, where unconstrained $L^2/H^1$ port bases violate M-matrix positivity (`C06`, `Exp 5A`) and small additive $z$-errors in distant rooms destroy $-\lambda \log z$ bounds unless paired with order-preserving subsolutions and cooperative Lyapunov scaling.
3. **MDP Lumpability & Bisimulation — Givan, Dean & Greig (2003); Ferns et al. (2004)**:
   - Standard transition-$L_1$ lumpability neither handles `tiny-Z` killed survival aliasing (`C12–C13`) nor yields task-specific coarse envelopes without full bisimulation; our `M2.3.1` framework constructs **query-certified upper/lower portal envelopes** rather than claiming a task-universal bisimulation quotient.
4. **Goal-Oriented DWR Error Estimation — Becker & Rannacher (2001)**:
   - Replaces asymptotic adjoint indicators with strict finite-sample primal–dual enclosures (`T2`) and empirically establishes why global-grid DWR (`M2.1` `STOP`) fails economically while localized portal-interface certification (`M2.3.1`) achieves `1.21x–2.39x` net speedups on `47^3–59^3` 3D grids.
