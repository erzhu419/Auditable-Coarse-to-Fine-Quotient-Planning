## 4. Differentiation From Prior Literature

1. **Port-Reduced Static Condensation & Multiscale FEM (Eftang & Patera 2013; Smetana & Patera 2016)**: Unconstrained Hilbert-norm ($H^1/L^2$) Petrov–Galerkin bases violate M-matrix positivity (`C06`, `Exp 5A`) and direct piecewise-constant Schur restrictions $S_{\Gamma\Gamma}L$ incur an $O(1/h^2)$ doorway-seam singularity in 3D. Our seam-free integral formulation $K_{\Gamma\Gamma} = A_{\Gamma\Gamma}^{-1}(-M)$ + Row-Coupled Howard subsolution $W_{\text{lo}}$ yields globally valid 3D M-matrix subsolutions ($A z_{\text{sub}} \le 0$) certifying closed-loop stochastic policies.
2. **MDP Lumpability & Bisimulation (Givan et al. 2003; Ferns et al. 2004)**: Raw transition $L_1$ tolerances fail on undiscounted killed diffusions via `tiny-Z` exit/cost aliasing (`C12–C13`) and cyclic amplification (`C15`), closed here via `audit_normalized_block_state_adequacy` and SSP/Lyapunov witnesses.
3. **Compositional LMDPs (Todorov 2009; Saxe et al. 2017; Ringstrom et al. 2020)**: Prior compositional LMDPs neither support zero-hint 3D portal discovery nor handle continual world-operator invalidation or $\exp(-V/\lambda)$ float64 underflow across deep 3D room chains (`where global Warm-ILU drifts by 134.2`), all resolved in `M2.3/M2.4`.
4. **Goal-Oriented DWR Error Estimation (Becker & Rannacher 2001)**: Replaces asymptotic exact-adjoint indicators with strict finite-sample enclosures (`T2`) and demonstrates why interface-localized DWR (`M2.3`) succeeds economically where global-grid DWR (`M2.1`) hits the monolithic-LU cliff.
