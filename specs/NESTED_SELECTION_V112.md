# V112: source-history validation selects whether to deploy an update

Freeze before new fitting or inner scoring. V111 separated extra optimization from the second-batch package; it did not establish stable accumulated gains. Replace unconditional deployment with one fixed validation rule, keeping the learner and its two candidate stages unchanged.

- Keep histories 11–14, widths 4/16, both existing queries, four-replica training labels, 121 features, HALF/FULL budgets 256000/512000 per source, the V110 UNIFORM_SHRINK loss, original initialization and 1000 steps per stage. FULL starts from its paired HALF parameters, freezes HALF normalization/gamma, resets Adam and adds 1000 steps. Actual first-batch membership and life/query/episode identities control fitting; original modulo-5 internal heldouts remain diagnostic only.
- For each outer target o, use only the other three source histories for selection. An inner model trains on two of those histories and validates on the third. Six unique source pairs cover all four outer folds; fit each pair once per width and stage, totaling 24 new fits/24000 steps. No target-history training records, statistics, decisions or references enter that outer fold's selection inputs. Pair fits reused by different outer folds are identical and counted once.
- Score each inner model only on the two histories absent from its training pair: 32 retained query roots per model. This yields 768 new inner decisions, 3840 candidate predictions and 38400 hidden activations. Original option-order and strict-positive action selection remain unchanged. Retain full inner predictions for both excluded histories; an outer selector consumes only its three legal source-validation cells.
- For each outer target, width and query, compute the FULL-minus-HALF selected reference utility on each of the three inner validation histories, average its eight roots, then average the three histories equally. Choose FULL exactly when this mean is greater than zero; otherwise choose HALF, including ties. Use pooled 32-replica references only. No A/B selection, optimizer-step search, additional candidate, width selection or target-based override. Sixteen query-specific choices consume 384 cached root comparisons and 768 cached decision lookups; these are arithmetic, not new neural predictions.

Derive 128 SELECTED_H4/SELECTED_H16 outer decisions by exact copies of the chosen HALF/FULL events, with chosen-model metadata retained. Do not refit or rescore outer models and do not present copied decisions as new model evaluations.
