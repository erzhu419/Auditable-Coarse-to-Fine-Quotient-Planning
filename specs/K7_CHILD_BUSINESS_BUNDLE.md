# K7 typed child business bundle

Contract `1.98.0` implements the child-owned first frame reserved by V0-103.
It consumes the exact fresh-exec V0-105 request replay, verifies the sealed
source/runtime and lifecycle-secret descriptors, reconstructs the registered
K7 `NO_PRIOR` schedule, and invokes the existing owned partial K7 runner once.

The emitted frame embeds the existing strict
`V075PortableOccurrenceEvidenceBundleV2` rather than creating a generic
document table. Raw replay therefore rechecks the portable schema, content
IDs, root topology and semantic role registry before binding the owned wrapper,
partial-native transcript and cold-cache documents to the exact V0-105 request.
Unknown fields, absent-root substitutions, request-as-result substitutions,
nested semantic mutations and crossed request coordinates fail closed.

Private generation values and RSA components are scanned in raw hexadecimal,
decoded-byte and base64 forms. The scan authority is process-local,
unpickleable and bound to the exact request, sealed-secret commitment, signer
registry and observer key. The signer and scanner derive from one secure key
read, removing the former two-open replacement window. A public verifier
cannot choose naked taint patterns. The child guards the Python stdlib
subprocess API, but does not claim complete OS process-spawn exclusion; the
parent cgroup boundary remains mandatory.

A real integration test executes the owned K7 construction path, freezes and
replays a genuine portable evidence bundle, reconstructs the exact portable
successor request, and rejects a rehashed nested semantic mutation.

This is only the child-owned first frame. It does not yet prove the complete
loaded-module graph, launch through the atomic cgroup/pidfd runtime, emit the
parent-owned accounting suffix, or independently replay the partial transcript
into formal accounting. `owned_partial_raw_semantic_replay_complete=false`,
and every CounterRecord, WorkVector, ComparisonVector, terminal, certificate,
scientific and official lock remains false. The next boundary is the
parent-owned atomic executor and accounting suffix.
