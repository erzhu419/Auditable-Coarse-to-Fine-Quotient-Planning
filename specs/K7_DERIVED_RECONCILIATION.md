# K7 derived reconciliation authority

Contract `2.0.24` freezes the exact formula and closure-dependency DAG for the
eight required V6 `derived_only` paths. Arithmetic replay alone is never
semantic evidence.

The process family is derived from the verified-nine `process.launches`
authorization and a fresh replay of the direct-PIDfd reap journal. The current
production fixture yields two successful exits and zero failures.

The solver family is derived only after replaying a complete five-stage owner
transcript and proving that neither `LOCAL_ATTEMPT` nor `DIRECT_FALLBACK` is in
the executed stage plan. It then yields zero attempts, successes and failures.

The route family remains blocked in this revision. A terminal status, child
bundle digest or typed owned wrapper does not independently prove the outcome.
Route reconciliation requires a successor authority that binds the fully
replayed owner transcript to the actual production `BUSINESS_RESULT` bytes,
SHA-256 and public-replay ID. Until then the three route paths carry
`ROUTE_TERMINAL_SEMANTIC_AUTHORITY_UNAVAILABLE`.

Formula, dependency, proof and readiness artifacts are centrally
domain-separated. Missing/extra values, negative or Boolean counts, cycles,
cross-occurrence evidence and post-issuance mutation fail closed. No formal
counter or vector is issued.
