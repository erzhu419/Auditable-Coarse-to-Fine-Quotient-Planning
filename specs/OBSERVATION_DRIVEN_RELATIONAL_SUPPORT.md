## Claim boundary and next Gate

V0-064 licenses automatic coordinate selection and anonymous abstract-support
proposal inside one fixed human relational vocabulary, followed by
certificate-triggered target-only statistical model construction and
context-local repeated planning. It does not license:

- D4 or another known group prior;
- named frontier rows;
- raw symbolization or primitive/operator invention;
- unknown outcome support;
- unseen graph geometry or broad generalization;
- cross-structural RAPM reuse;
- independent-algorithm verification;
- sample efficiency or a sample-tax reduction;
- official execution, scalar cost, or break-even.

`WORKLOAD_ECONOMICS_GATE`, `COUNTER_COMPLETENESS_GATE`, and
`SAMPLE_EFFICIENCY_GATE` remain `NOT_RUN`.

The next construction Gate must preregister multiple graph geometries or a
second domain, run the same source-only proposal and certificate-triggered
partial/statistical recovery, and include explicit no-transfer/OOD controls.
Only after those acquisition traces exist should a Laplace-style heuristic
operator or KG-OP-style meta-prior be evaluated for sample-tax reduction.
