# V70: finite observation compilation

Frozen before the main run, 2026-09-13. V69 learned compositional dynamics
and its 32 H3/H4 roots, 14 queries and 60 retained observations stay fixed.
The question is whether direct observation encoding removes reconstruction
cost while retaining every V69 model, plan and covered observation mapping.

Compare FULL_MAP, COMPOSED_MAP and COMPOSED_DAG. Both maps retain exact active
(horizon, board) keys. The DAG compiles newly generated COMPOSED labels into
rank-valued tests in cell order 0..15; identical suffix subtrees share nodes.
Every accepting path tests all 16 ranks. Unsupported observations return None.
The DAG is a finite support compiler, not a learned generalization rule.

Rebuild FULL and COMPOSED once per retained root with V69's frozen source rule
and unchanged 200,000 concrete-state limit; alternate their order. No refit,
target acquisition, new roots or new ground audit is performed. Use the newly
built encoding to construct each router; independently recover the expected
mapping from V69's saved exact joint kernels only after method execution.
Exact core payload equality, all plan policies/values/counts, all active
observations and all 60 retained routes must pass. Otherwise V69 scientific
evidence is not inherited. Valid unsupported-board probes check membership
rejection only. A resource limit remains a recorded incomplete result.

Method cost starts from the frozen source rule and includes construction,
payload/label preparation, router compilation, complete package serialization,
one fresh Python process loading the package, index construction, 14-query
planning, retained observation routing, writing all solutions, and cleanup.
Rotate the three downstream arms by case index. The worker is the actual
method use and portable validation simultaneously; do not replan a second
time solely for validation. Its complete subprocess wall includes startup and
output writing; internal load/index/plan/route times are explanatory subitems,
not additions to that wall. Count complete kernel-plus-router bytes, not just
DAG bytes. Charge identical COMPOSED upstream cost once to each candidate in
comparisons, but record it only once as actual campaign work. Retained V69
source acquisition/fit is historical and excluded from fresh timing ratios.

After all arms of a case are saved, compare their kernels and solutions with
V69 and audit every active observation independently. This verification and
campaign setup/cleanup remain separately visible in campaign wall time.
The runner snapshots its source and protocol, preserves failed attempts, and
does not tune or rerun the main cohort to improve timing. Six targeted toy
tests cover exact membership/roundtrip and independent corruption detection.

Report correctness first, then total measured time and complete package size
against FULL and the simple COMPOSED map. Adoption of a direct route requires
exact preservation and zero dynamics expansion during encoding; any efficiency
claim must follow measured total cost. A DAG slower or larger than a map is
retained as that result. This study does not change concrete closure generation,
privileged V69 supervision, U005 failure, the closed V68 classifier or U006.
